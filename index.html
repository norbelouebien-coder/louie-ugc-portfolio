Spec ad breakdown · AI UGC

How I'd sell whey protein

An AI UGC ad I made on my own to show how I think: who it's for, why this format, and two openings ready to test. Spec ad, not a paid collab.

01 The brief Whey protein for gym-goers who train hard but struggle to hit their daily protein. The goal of this ad: first-time purchases from people already in the gym.
02 The angle Same body, two openings. A real-time shake demo filmed at the gym: one scoop, cold water, shake, sip, with the proof points (24g protein, 120 calories) said out loud. It feels like a gym buddy showing his routine, not a commercial, and the tub stays in frame the whole time.
03 Two hooks to test
Hook A · Speed promise “My whole post-gym routine takes thirty seconds. Watch.” Promises a quick payoff and tells the viewer to keep watching, so the demo itself becomes the reason to stay.
Hook B · Call-out “Stop overthinking your post-workout shake. This is all you need.” Calls out the viewer's frustration with supplement overload and offers a simple answer.
04 How I'd scale it Run both openings, keep the winner, then build new versions on it: a taste test, a post-workout routine and a "how much protein do you actually need" explainer. That's exactly how the monthly retainer works.
